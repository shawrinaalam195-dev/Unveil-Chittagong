Explore the Beauty of Cox's Bazar

Introduction

Cox's Bazar, located on the southeastern coast of Bangladesh, is renowned for having the longest unbroken sea beach in the world. With its golden sandy shores, vibrant beach culture, and rich history, it's an ideal destination for both relaxation and adventure. Whether you're looking to bask in the sun, explore the local wildlife, or dive into the city's culture, Cox's Bazar has something for everyone.

Top Tourist Attractions

Inani Beach

Inani Beach, with its pristine sand and crystal-clear waters, is perfect for a peaceful retreat. It offers great views and a serene atmosphere.

Laboni Beach

Located near the town center, Laboni Beach is popular for its lively atmosphere, street food, and fun activities. A great place for tourists to enjoy the sea.

Himchari Waterfall

Located near the coast, Himchari Waterfall is a beautiful spot surrounded by lush greenery, perfect for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Cox's Bazar Railway Station

Cox's Bazar Railway Station offers easy access to the city by rail, and its historical charm makes it a unique destination for tourists arriving by train.

Radiant Fish World

Radiant Fish World is a stunning aquatic park where visitors can observe and learn about various marine species. It's a perfect stop for families and nature lovers.

Ramu Buddhist Temple

Ramu is home to several Buddhist temples, and the Ramu Buddhist Temple is a significant one for those looking to explore local culture and heritage.

How to Reach Cox's Bazar

You can reach Cox's Bazar by bus from Dhaka, Chittagong, or other major cities. The journey takes around 10-12 hours by road. Alternatively, there are flights from Dhaka to Cox's Bazar, making travel more convenient. Upon arrival, local taxis, rickshaws, and hired cars can help you explore the area.

Famous Foods

Grilled Lobster

A deliciously grilled lobster, seasoned with local spices, offering a smoky and rich flavor.

Dry Fish (Shutki)

Dried fish, a popular delicacy in coastal areas, often served with rice, offering a savory and tangy taste.

Rupchada Fry

A crispy fried fish dish made with rupchada (small local fish), seasoned with spices to enhance its natural flavors.

Sea Food Platter

A delightful assortment of fresh seafood, including shrimp, crab, and various fish, perfect for seafood lovers.

Different Types of Vorta

A variety of mashed dishes, including eggplant, potato, and fish, flavored with mustard oil, chilies, and local spices.

Tender Coconut Ice Cream

A refreshing ice cream made from tender coconut, offering a creamy and tropical taste that’s perfect for a hot day.

Where to Stay

Cox's Bazar offers various accommodation options ranging from budget hotels to luxury resorts:

  • Hotel Sea Palace: Located near Laboni Beach, offering comfortable rooms with sea views.
  • Best Western Cox's Bazar: A premium hotel with modern amenities and beach access.
  • Mermaid Resort: A beachside resort offering luxurious stays and various recreational activities.
  • Ocean Paradise Hotel: A great choice for travelers looking for a peaceful retreat with beautiful views.
