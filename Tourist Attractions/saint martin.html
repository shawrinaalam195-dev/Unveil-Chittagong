Explore the Beauty of Saint Martin's Island

Introduction

Saint Martin's Island, the only coral island of Bangladesh, is a tropical paradise located in the Bay of Bengal. Known for its pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant marine life, it's the perfect destination for a relaxing getaway. Whether you're a nature lover, a beach enthusiast, or an adventure seeker, Saint Martin's offers something for everyone.

Top Tourist Attractions

Saint Martin's Beach

The main attraction of the island, known for its white sandy beaches and crystal-clear water, perfect for sunbathing and swimming.

Coral Reef

Explore the vibrant coral reefs that surround the island. A snorkeling paradise for marine life enthusiasts.

Visit the Fishermen's Village

Experience the local culture by visiting the traditional fishermen's village, where you can observe the daily lives of the island’s inhabitants.

Patharghata

Explore the rocky areas of Patharghata, where you can enjoy beautiful views of the sea and the surrounding landscape.

Chhera Dwip

A small, tranquil island near Saint Martin's, known for its serene environment and stunning sunset views.

Marine Life Watching

Engage in marine life watching, where you can spot various species of fish, turtles, and other marine creatures.

How to Reach Saint Martin's Island

Saint Martin's Island is accessible by boat from Cox's Bazar. The journey takes approximately 2-3 hours, depending on the weather conditions. There are regular boat services available from the Teknaf Port. The island is also accessible by small fishing boats or local cruises.

Famous Foods

Grilled Fish

Freshly caught fish grilled with local spices, a popular and flavorful dish on the island.

Fried Shrimp

Deliciously crispy fried shrimp, seasoned with a mix of spices, a local favorite for seafood lovers.

Coconut Pudding

A sweet and creamy pudding made from coconut milk, offering a rich, tropical flavor.

Ilish Bhorta

Smoked hilsa fish mashed with mustard oil, green chilies, and onions, a traditional Bengali dish.

Coconut Water

Fresh coconut water served straight from the coconut, a refreshing drink to cool down under the sun.

Macher Jhol

A flavorful fish curry made with fresh catch, cooked in aromatic spices and served with steamed rice.

Where to Stay

There are a variety of accommodation options available on Saint Martin's Island, ranging from budget-friendly guesthouses to more luxurious resorts:

  • Saint Martin's Resort: A popular choice for a comfortable stay, offering rooms with stunning views of the ocean.
  • Coral View Resort: Located near the beach, offering peaceful stays with modern amenities.
  • Blue Marine Resort: An eco-friendly resort with simple, cozy accommodations close to the beach.
  • Fisherman's Retreat: A budget-friendly guesthouse with direct access to the beach, offering basic but comfortable rooms.
