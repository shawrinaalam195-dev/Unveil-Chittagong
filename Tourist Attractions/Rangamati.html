Explore the Beauty of Rangamati

Introduction

Rangamati, the "Lake City" of Bangladesh, is a breathtaking district surrounded by hills, lakes, and vibrant indigenous cultures. It's a perfect destination for those looking to enjoy nature, boat rides, and unique tribal heritage.

Top Tourist Attractions

Kaptai Lake

The largest man-made lake in Bangladesh, offering stunning boat rides and breathtaking views.

Shuvolong Waterfall

A beautiful waterfall accessible only by boat, located deep within the Rangamati hills.

Hanging Bridge

A famous suspension bridge offering scenic views of the lake and surrounding hills.

Rajbon Bihar

A sacred Buddhist monastery known for its peaceful ambiance and unique architecture.

Dhuppani Waterfall

Dhuppani Waterfall is a breathtaking, less-explored cascade in Bandarban, Bangladesh, surrounded by dense forests and accessible through a challenging yet scenic trekking route. .

Polwel park

Polwel Park in Chittagong is a scenic getaway with a lake, lush greenery, and family-friendly attractions.

How to Reach Rangamati

You can reach Rangamati by bus from Dhaka or Chittagong. The journey takes around 7-9 hours by road. To explore Kaptai and other parts of Rangamati, boats and local transport (Chander Gari) are available.

Famous Foods

Pachon(Flavorful mixed vegetable)

Pachon is a traditional Chakma dish. It's especially famous during festivals, family gatherings, and traditional celebrations in Rangamati's tribal communities.

Smoked Hilsa

Fresh hilsa fish smoked over an open flame, giving it a rich, deep flavor, often served with rice and spicy chutneys.

Pineapple Pickle

A tangy and spicy pickle made from Rangamati’s locally grown pineapples, infused with mustard oil and red chili.

Crab Masala

Freshwater crabs from the lakes, cooked in a rich and spicy coconut-based curry, making it a flavorful delicacy.

Pumpkin Flower Fritters

Crispy deep-fried pumpkin flowers, coated in a light batter, often enjoyed as a snack with tea.

Bamboo Shoot Beef Curry

A fragrant beef curry cooked with tender bamboo shoots, giving it a distinct earthy aroma and taste.

Where to Stay

Rangamati offers a variety of accommodations for different budgets:

  • Rangamati Parjatan Complex: A government-run motel with modern facilities.
  • Peda Ting Ting Resort: A serene island resort with a beautiful lake view.
  • Hotel Green Castle: A comfortable hotel offering great hospitality.
  • Aronnok Holiday Resort: A nature-friendly resort surrounded by greenery.
