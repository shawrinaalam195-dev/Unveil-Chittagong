City Glimpse: Discover the Charm of Chittagong

Introduction

Chittagong, officially known as Chattogram, is Bangladesh's second-largest city and its principal seaport. Nestled between the Bay of Bengal and the Chittagong Hill Tracts, this city offers a unique blend of natural beauty, rich history, and vibrant culture. From bustling markets to serene beaches, Chittagong has something for every traveler.

Top Tourist Attractions

Shah Amanat International Airport

The main gateway to Chattogram, this airport connects the city to various domestic and international destinations.

Patenga Sea Beach

A popular spot for locals and tourists alike, offering serene views of the Bay of Bengal, especially mesmerizing during sunset.

Foy’s Lake

A man-made lake surrounded by hills, ideal for boat rides, picnics, and enjoying amusement park facilities.

Naval Beach

A tranquil beach area perfect for leisurely walks and enjoying the coastal breeze.

Karnaphuli Tunnel

Bangladesh's first underwater tunnel, connecting the city's two sides beneath the Karnaphuli River, showcasing modern engineering marvels.

Chattogram Commonwealth War Cemetery

A serene and well-maintained cemetery honoring the soldiers who sacrificed their lives during World War II.

How to Reach Chattogram

Chattogram is accessible by various modes of transportation:

  • By Air: Shah Amanat International Airport connects Chattogram to major domestic cities like Dhaka (approximately 1 hour) and international destinations such as Kolkata, Dubai, and Muscat.
  • By Train: Regular train services from Dhaka to Chattogram take approximately 6-7 hours, offering scenic views of the countryside.
  • By Road: Well-maintained highways connect Dhaka to Chattogram, with bus journeys taking around 5-6 hours.

Famous Foods

Mezban Beef

A signature Chattogram dish, Mezban is a spicy beef curry traditionally served during community feasts and social gatherings.

Street Food

Pani puri, fuchka, chowmein & chotpoti—taste of every corner street.

Kala Bhuna

A dark, rich beef curry cooked with an array of spices, resulting in a deep, flavorful taste unique to Chattogram.

Beef Tehari

In Chattogram, Beef Tehari is often called "Orosh-er Biryani" because it's commonly served at religious gatherings (Orosh). It’s rich, flavorful, and cooked with mustard oil, turmeric, and aromatic spices.

Shutki Bhuna

Spicy stir-fried dried fish cooked with onions, garlic, and chilies, offering an intense flavor popular among locals.

Dudh Puli

A traditional Bengali dessert made of rice flour dumplings filled with coconut and jaggery, simmered in thickened milk.
