Explore the Majestic Sajek Valley

Introduction

Sajek, located in the remote hills of Bangladesh, is a paradise for nature lovers. Known for its misty hills, serene valleys, and breathtaking landscapes, Sajek offers an unforgettable experience. With the charm of tribal culture and the cool mountain breeze, it's the perfect getaway for peace seekers and adventure enthusiasts alike.

Our website serves as your guide to explore the hidden gems of Sajek Valley, offering detailed information on attractions, travel tips, and routes, ensuring you make the most of your adventure.

Top Tourist Attractions

Konglak Hill

A serene hilltop offering spectacular views of the surrounding valleys and an ideal spot for nature lovers.

Risang Waterfall

A breathtaking waterfall surrounded by lush greenery, providing a perfect setting for relaxation and photography.

Helipad

The Helipad offers a unique aerial view of Sajek, allowing visitors to enjoy the beauty of the valley from above.

Stone Garden

A unique landscape filled with large, beautiful stones and rocks, perfect for hiking and exploration.

Sajek Viewpoint

The most famous viewpoint in Sajek, offering mesmerizing views of the hills and valleys, especially at sunrise and sunset.

Sajek River

A peaceful river winding through the mountains, perfect for a tranquil boat ride and nature immersion.

How to Reach Sajek

Sajek is accessible by road from Khagrachari or Rangamati. The journey takes around 4-6 hours by car or jeep, depending on road conditions. Once you reach the area, local jeeps (Chander Gari) are available for travel within the valley.

Famous Foods

Wild Honey Tea

A refreshing tea infused with organic wild honey sourced from the nearby hills.

Hill Bamboo Shoot Curry

A traditional dish made with fresh bamboo shoots, offering a unique earthy flavor.

Turmeric Leaf Grilled Fish

Fresh fish wrapped in turmeric leaves and grilled to perfection, enhancing its aroma.

Chapali Kabab

A delicious indigenous-style kabab made with minced meat and aromatic spices.

Ginger Chicken

A flavorful chicken dish cooked with fresh ginger and traditional spices.

Hill Banana Fry

Special bananas from the hill tracts, deep-fried for a crispy and delightful snack.

Where to Stay

Sajek offers various accommodations ranging from budget-friendly guesthouses to luxury resorts:

  • Sajek Valley Resort: A serene resort with breathtaking views of the valley.
  • Sky View Resort: A resort offering panoramic views of the mountains and valleys, perfect for nature lovers.
  • Sajek Resort & Spa: A luxurious resort with spa facilities, providing a perfect blend of comfort and nature.
  • Sajek Grand Resort: A grand resort offering premium services and stunning views of the Sajek Valley.
  • Ruilui Resort: A peaceful resort with a focus on sustainability and eco-tourism.
  • Megpunji Resort: A nature-centric resort offering an authentic tribal experience with modern amenities.
