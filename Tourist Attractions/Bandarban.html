Explore the Beauty of Bandarban

Introduction

Bandarban, a picturesque hill district in Bangladesh, is known for its stunning landscapes, tribal culture, and vibrant natural beauty. It's the perfect destination for nature lovers, adventure seekers, and those looking to experience authentic cultural heritage.With iconic destinations like Keokradong, Sangu River, and Nafakhum Falls, it offers unparalleled beauty and tranquility.

Our website serves as your guide to explore the hidden gems of Bandarban, offering detailed information on attractions, travel tips, and routes, ensuring you make the most of your adventure.

Top Tourist Attractions

Nilgiri Hills

Experience the clouds and breathtaking views from Nilgiri Hills. It's a must-see spot for trekkers and nature enthusiasts, offering panoramic views of lush green hills.

Boga Lake

A mystical lake surrounded by hills, perfect for trekking and camping. It’s one of the highest lakes in Bangladesh and offers a tranquil environment for visitors.

Debotakhum

Debotakhum is a spectacular canyon with crystal-clear water, surrounded by towering cliffs. Visitors can explore it by raft, making it an adventurous yet serene experience in the heart of nature.

Sangu River

Sangu River is a hidden gem of Bandarban, flowing through misty valleys and lush green landscapes.

Mirinja Valley

Mirinja Valley offers breathtaking panoramic views of the rolling hills and lush green landscapes. It’s a perfect spot for nature lovers and photographers.

Keokradong

Keokradong is the country’s second-highest mountain, where misty trails and rugged beauty create an unforgettable trek.

How to Reach Bandarban

You can reach Bandarban by bus from Dhaka or Chittagong. The journey takes approximately 6-8 hours by road. For local sightseeing, you can use Chander Gari (local 4x4 vehicles), which are available for rent for a day trip or a longer stay.

Famous Foods

Bamboo Chicken

A local delicacy cooked in bamboo, infused with traditional spices and herbs.

Jum Rice

A unique type of rice grown by the indigenous tribes of Bandarban, usually served with fish or meat curry.

Marma-style Thai Soup

A flavorful soup with a mix of Thai and Marma spices, often served with rice noodles.

Chingri Bhorta

A shrimp dish prepared with mustard oil and spices, offering a rich and tangy flavor.

Mushroom Curry

A popular tribal dish made with wild mushrooms and aromatic spices, giving it a unique earthy flavor.

Hill Bamboo Shoots Salad

A refreshing salad made from tender bamboo shoots, mixed with local herbs and spices.

Where to Stay

From budget hotels to luxury resorts, Bandarban offers a variety of accommodation options for travelers:

  • Sairu Hill Resort: A luxurious resort located 18 km from Bandarban town, perched atop the hills, offering breathtaking views of clouds and lush greenery.
  • Hotel Hill Town: A popular choice among tourists, offering comfortable rooms with stunning views of the surrounding hills.
  • Chimbuk Resort: Located near Chimbuk Hill, this resort offers a peaceful retreat with modern amenities.
  • Ruma Resort: Situated near Ruma Valley, an ideal base for trekking and adventure activities.
  • Nilgiri Eco Resort: An eco-friendly resort blending nature with comfort in Nilgiri Hills.
