About Chittagong

Chittagong, the bustling port city of Bangladesh, is a hidden gem for travelers seeking a mix of nature, history, and culture. Nestled between the Bay of Bengal and lush green hills, this vibrant city offers breathtaking landscapes, golden beaches, and rich heritage. Explore the serene beauty of Cox’s Bazar and Patenga Beach, venture into the hill tracts for an adventure in Bandarban and Rangamati, and discover centuries-old mosques, temples, and colonial-era architecture that reflect Chittagong’s diverse history. Take a boat ride through the tranquil Kaptai Lake, hike to Nafakhum Waterfall, or witness the grandeur of the Karnaphuli River. Whether you’re looking for relaxation or adventure, Chittagong offers an unforgettable experience. Come and experience the timeless charm of Chittagong – where nature meets tradition!
